BRD-01 · The “Currently into” and prompt cards

Click through each phone: tap the cards, type on the fake keyboard, pick things. “Reset” puts a phone back to its start.

What we decided: tapping Currently into shows a small text field (free text), mid-month edits are fine, and old values are remembered so you can look back at each month. The prompt opens the composer with the prompt text visible; you can answer with writing, a photo, or a post you already made. Only one answer; deleting it brings back the dashed “+”. Tapping an answered card scrolls to the post and gives it a soft pulse that fades.
