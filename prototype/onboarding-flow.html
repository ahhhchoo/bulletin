“Bulletin” Would Like to Send You Notifications

Notifications may include alerts and sounds.

The three tips

    First-post tip

    Two ways to nudge a first post. Switch and start over.

    What I decided, and what’s still open

    • No full-screen steps. The board is the onboarding. The tips sit on top of the real header cards, composer and tab bar, so people learn where things live while they use them.
    • Tip 1, “into”: points at the card. Empty, the card has a system-blue outline and a blinking caret after “Tap to set it”, so it reads as a field. Tapping turns it into a text field in place, the same as editing it later. Return saves it and moves on. No suggestions.
    • Tip 2, first post: the composer (A) or the prompt card (B). Writing something makes the sticky land on today.
    • Tip 3, invite: points at the Friends tab, because that’s where the guest list lives. “Invite someone” opens the share sheet with a private single-use link, good for one week. “Later” just closes it.
    • One tip at a time, each with its own “Not now”. Nothing blocks the board, and once a tip is dismissed it doesn’t come back.
    • Open: when does tip 3 appear? needs a call Right after the first post, as shown. Alternatively wait a day, so the invite comes when they’re already back.
    • Open: does the dot stay? needs a call If someone dismisses the invite tip, a small blue dot on the Friends tab could say there’s still a first invite to send.
    • Notifications are asked when the first note arrives (the last step on the left, which would fire hours or days later in the real app). A small tip over the note asks “Want a heads-up?”, and only “Turn on notifications” opens Apple’s system alert. If someone taps “Not now” we never show Apple’s alert, so the one real chance isn’t spent.
    • Open: asking again. needs a call After “Not now” I’d ask once more on the next note, then leave it to Settings. After “Don’t Allow” never again.
    • Open: invited people. needs a call People who arrive through a link probably get a shorter version of this (no invite tip). Their welcome (ONB-01, ONB-04) isn’t designed yet.
