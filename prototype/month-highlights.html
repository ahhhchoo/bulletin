November Highlights: drag to tear

Your page, now with more in it. Scroll the page to read it, then drag the scissors along the dotted line to tear it off. Everything on it comes from what you made. Nothing social, no scores.

Highlights page

Scroll the page, then tear. After the tear: tiny shake, shelf tab ripples, bounces and sparkles, badge pops on.

  • What made you smile
  • Your favorite picture
  • First and last pin of the month
  • Busiest day, with a dot on a mini timeline
  • Longest streak (only if there was one)
  • Longest essay (tap to read)
  • Biggest sticky, at full size
  • The month in sticky colors
  • A closing line (only if it's a good comparison)
