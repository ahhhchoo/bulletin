Friends: shared blocks

Each friend is a block: their name, the last thing they posted and when. A blue dot and a white block mean you haven't opened their bulletin yet; once you do, it goes gray. The four phones differ only in how you manage your guest list. Tap a block to open it (the dot goes away), tap Edit to try each version.

Not built in the app yet. Pick the guest list you like (or mix them) and I'll implement it in SwiftUI.
