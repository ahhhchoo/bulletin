ARC-01 — Shelf, album cards

Round two, from the new reference: each sealed month is an album, a colored card with a collage of its photos and a frosted band across the bottom, the title and date under it, and the friends who left notes as a row of faces. The month's color is its sticky color. Photos here are samples. Arrow buttons stand in for the "+", since a sealed month can't take new posts. One locked month in each. Tap the deck in I; the rest are static.
