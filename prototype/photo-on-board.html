On the board, then the thread

You picked the thread (option A) for the detailed view. This is how it connects to the board. On the board, a post only shows its newest three notes, tucked beside the photo; the rest hide behind a “+N” pill. Tap the photo, a note, or the pill and you land in the thread. Everything is live: drag the green sticky from the dock onto the photo, then write.

What to look at: the photo lights up with a dashed outline when you’re over it · a pink corner on a note means the owner replied · “+6 notes” appears once there are more than three · the board never grows taller however many notes arrive.
