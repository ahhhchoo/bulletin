Month's end: recap, then tear it off

The first time you open the app after a month ends, last month shows up as one more page of your bulletin. Read it, then tear it off the board. It drops into the shelf as an issue and the new month starts clean.

Recap + tear-off

The recap is made of the same pieces as your board: chat bubbles, a timeline, pinned posts.

How it plays

  1. When. The first time you open the app after the last day of the month (so, on the 1st). It doesn't pop up at midnight on its own.
  2. Read it. Your month in the app's own voice: a bubble asks, another answers with your counts, then your prompt answer. Below, your most-noted photo and a favorite sticky hang on the timeline like any other day.
  3. Tear it. Drag the scissors along the dashed edge. Let go early and it snaps back.
  4. The aftermath. The phone gives a little shake and paper scraps fall from the tear. The page comes free with a ragged edge and arcs down to the shelf tab, which ripples, bounces and sparkles as the badge pops on.
  5. December arrives. "November" rolls up into "December", today's dot pulses, the empty line fades in, and the compose bubble springs up with its cursor blinking.

Nothing social in it, no visit counts. Only what you made.
