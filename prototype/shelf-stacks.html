ARC-01 — Shelf, in colored stacks

Five takes on the shelf, inspired by the reference: overlapping rounded cards, big numbers, pills. Every month wears its own sticky color (banana, celery, shrimp, shark, grape, orange), so the shelf keeps each month's look. One locked month is in each (plain gray, no title). Numbers are JetBrains Mono, text is SF Pro. Tap the cards, spines and tiles: A has a press, B / D / E react.
