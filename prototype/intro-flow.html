ONB-01 · Intro, one paragraph

One paragraph that builds as you go, with no highlighting. It stops after “Jot down what’s on your mind,” until the sticky appears. Then the sticky goes, the page scrolls up a little, and “journal like you usually do,” arrives with the long note. The note goes, the page scrolls, “keep things you want to remember,” arrives with the photo. The photo goes, the last line arrives, and the page scrolls all the way up to sign-in.
