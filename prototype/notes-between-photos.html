Stickies between photos

Stickies are full size and land where you let go. A pink dashed sticky shows the spot, and the photo it belongs to gets a dashed outline. If the photo already has notes, the new one goes on top of them: the newest note is the one you read, with the older ones peeking out from underneath. A sticky may tuck a little over a photo’s edge, never its middle or its caption. If you drop it on a photo, it lands in the nearest open space, and a dotted line ties it back to its photo.

Rules in play: a note belongs to the nearest photo (within about 90pt) · full-size stickies · lands where dropped, or the nearest open space · newest on top, older ones underneath with a +N count · a dotted line connects a sticky that landed away from its photo · one note per person per photo · the thread shows them all.
