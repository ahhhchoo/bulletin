ARC-01 — Shelf, cascading cards

Round three, from the Spotify clips: months are bold, tilted cards in a cascade. They straighten as they reach the middle of the screen and tilt away at the edges. Tapping one opens a full color sheet with the cover photo, a big title and pill buttons. Scroll inside a phone and tap a month. Colors are the sticky colors; the locked month is gray. Type is SF Pro with JetBrains Mono for dates and numbers.
