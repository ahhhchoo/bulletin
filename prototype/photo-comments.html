Detailed view · notes on a photo

Three ways to show the notes people leave on a photo, once it’s opened. Each note belongs to one post. Try it as a visitor (leave a note or a sticker) and as the owner (reply to a note, tuck one away), and flip between 1, 5 and 15 notes to see how each layout copes.

Rules I assumed (tell me if any are wrong): one note per person per post, which they can take back · only the owner replies, one reply per note, one level deep · one sticker per person per post, shown as a small pile with no counts · notes show who they’re from.
