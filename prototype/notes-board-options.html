Stickies on the board: four options

Same four photos, same full-size stickies, newest on top with older notes underneath. The difference is where a photo’s notes sit. Drag the green sticky from the dock onto any photo in any phone to try it; the dashed sticky shows where it will go. Reset puts the notes back.

Common rules: a note belongs to the nearest photo · only the newest note is readable on the board, the rest are underneath with a +N count · tapping a pile would open the thread · one note per person per photo.
