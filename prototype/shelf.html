ARC-01 · Shelf options

The shelf is where sealed months live: each one becomes a zine cover with the month’s look, its title, and a cover chosen from your most-noted post. Tap any month to open it read-only (ARC-02) and flip between months with the arrows. Four ways to lay the shelf out. Switch to a friend’s shelf to see locked covers, and try a long shelf to see how each one holds up.
