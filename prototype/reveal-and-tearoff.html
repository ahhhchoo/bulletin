Answer to reveal, and tear-off at month's end

Two ideas for making bulletins feel more alive. Everything here is tappable or draggable. Use the replay link under each phone to run it again.

1. Answer to revealA friend's answer to the monthly prompt (and the sticky it came from) stays hidden until you answer too. Two ways it could look.

2. Tear-off at month's endOn the 1st, last month is finished: it comes off the board, goes to the shelf as an issue, and you start a clean page. Two ways it could feel.

Not built in the app yet. Tell me which of each pair you like and I'll bring it into SwiftUI.
