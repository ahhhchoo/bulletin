Post detail: notes that stack up as you scroll

Version C, reworked the way you described: the comments aren't all there at once. The photo stays, you scroll, and each note arrives and settles onto the pile. Two ways the pile can look. Scroll inside the phones to try it.

Comments:

C1A pile

Each note lands on the same spot, a little crooked, on top of the last. You can still see the corners of the ones underneath, so it reads as a growing pile.

C2A tidy stack

Each new note slides up, and the older ones tuck upward behind it, a strip of each still showing, like cards in a wallet. Straighter and calmer than the pile.

How both behave

  1. Open. The photo grows in, then the first comment is taped on below, same as your frame.
  2. Scroll. The photo shrinks a little to make room, and the next note slides up from below as you go. It follows your finger: stop halfway and it stops halfway.
  3. Count. "2 of 5" and dots keep track, so you know how many are left.
  4. One comment? Nothing scrolls. It looks exactly like your frame.
