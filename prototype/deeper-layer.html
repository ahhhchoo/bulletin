Comments in a deeper layer

Your bulletin is about you, so friends’ notes stay out of the way. Nothing social shows on the board. Open a photo and you see just the photo. Only if you reach further down does the conversation appear, under the picture, on its own layer. To add a note, drag a sticky up from the bottom edge into that layer. Both phones are live: tap the first photo to start.

The flow: board (clean) → tap a photo → detailed view (just the photo; a sticky peeks up from the bottom edge only if there is something below, or if you’re visiting a friend) → pull up or tap it → the layer below slides up under a smaller photo → drag a sticky up from the bottom to leave a note. On your own board you only read, reply and tuck away; you never get a prompt to add notes to your own photos.
